## What I build

My current independent product work is **World Publishing Houses (WPH)**, a multilingual book-discovery platform. I work on research workflows, source-backed records and provenance, reviewer queues, evaluation, and safety controls. The core application repository is private; the case study and public examples below show selected work.

- **Agent and retrieval workflows:** connect a question to relevant records and source evidence, then make uncertainty visible.
- **Evaluation:** use golden cases and explicit checks for unsupported claims, factual consistency, and appropriate handling of unverified records.
- **Reliable operation:** add traces, failure visibility, human review, and deterministic controls around higher-risk decisions.

## Selected work

### [WPH LLM Evaluation](https://github.com/tetianakravchuk/wph-llm-eval)

A runnable evaluation harness for source-grounded answers. It includes deterministic checks, golden examples, regression tests, and an optional LLM-as-judge path. The cases target unsupported attributions, factual errors, language-code confusion, and unverified information presented as fact.

[View code](https://github.com/tetianakravchuk/wph-llm-eval) ·
[Evaluation case study](https://tetianakravchuk.com/projects/ai-evaluation-observability/)

### [AI Reliability Engineering Examples](https://github.com/tetianakravchuk/ai-reliability-engineering-examples)

A standalone PostgreSQL example of atomic observation ingestion and optimistic checkpoint updates. Integration tests exercise rollback, competing workers, interruption, and duplicate-write protection against a disposable database.

[View code](https://github.com/tetianakravchuk/ai-reliability-engineering-examples)

### [World Publishing Houses AI Platform](https://tetianakravchuk.com/projects/wph-ai-platform/)

An independent product case study covering multilingual discovery, source-backed ingestion, research workflows, reviewer tools, evaluation, and controlled promotion.

[Case study](https://tetianakravchuk.com/projects/wph-ai-platform/) ·
[Live platform](https://worldpublishinghouses.com) ·
[Video walkthroughs](https://tetianakravchuk.com/projects/wph-ai-platform/#technical-deep-dives)

### [Housing Price Prediction](https://github.com/tetianakravchuk/PredictHousingPrices)
